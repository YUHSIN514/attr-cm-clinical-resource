## Role of genetic testing

Once ATTR is established, TTR genetic testing distinguishes hereditary ATTR from wild-type ATTR and can inform family evaluation and phenotype interpretation.

Variant pages will clearly separate established pathogenic variants from variants of uncertain significance.
