## Why this step matters

A bone scintigraphy result should not be interpreted in isolation when AL amyloidosis remains possible. The diagnostic work-up typically incorporates assessment for a monoclonal protein process.

## Common laboratory components

- Serum immunofixation
- Urine immunofixation
- Serum free light-chain assay

Renal function and other clinical factors can affect free light-chain interpretation. Abnormal results may require hematology assessment and, depending on the overall clinical picture, tissue confirmation with definitive amyloid typing.
