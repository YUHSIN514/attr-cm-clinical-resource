## Planned structure

Guidelines will be indexed by issuing organization and year, with direct links to the original publication.

Each entry will identify:

- Organization / society
- Publication year
- Diagnostic recommendations
- Imaging recommendations
- Genetic-testing recommendations
- Treatment recommendations, when within scope

Older documents will remain accessible but clearly labeled by publication year.
