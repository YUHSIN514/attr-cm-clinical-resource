## Clinical role

99mTc-PYP bone scintigraphy（PYP 骨顯像）is an important component of ATTR-CM diagnosis when interpreted together with monoclonal protein testing and the clinical/imaging phenotype.

## Interpretation principles

Planar uptake grade alone is not the entire study. SPECT or SPECT/CT can help confirm myocardial localization and reduce misinterpretation caused by blood pool or adjacent bone activity.

## When uncertainty remains

Discordant imaging, an abnormal monoclonal protein evaluation, or a phenotype not adequately explained by ATTR may warrant additional imaging or tissue diagnosis with amyloid typing.
