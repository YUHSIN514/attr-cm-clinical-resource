## Echocardiography

Echocardiography defines ventricular wall thickness, chamber geometry, systolic and diastolic function, valve findings and longitudinal strain patterns. Findings may raise suspicion but are not independently diagnostic of ATTR-CM.

## Cardiac magnetic resonance

CMR can characterize tissue using late gadolinium enhancement, native T1 and extracellular volume. It can support the diagnosis of cardiac amyloidosis and help evaluate competing causes of hypertrophic phenotype.

## Nuclear imaging

Bone-avid tracer scintigraphy has a specific role in ATTR-CM diagnostic pathways. Acquisition technique, myocardial localization and monoclonal protein testing are integral to interpretation.
