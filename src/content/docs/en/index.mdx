## Editorial approach

This site is designed as a **source-first clinical reference**. Content is organized by clinical question rather than by product or treatment brand. Evidence types are identified whenever possible, and summaries are separated from interpretation.

The first version is intentionally small. It establishes the information architecture, mobile navigation, site search and article format before additional literature is added.
