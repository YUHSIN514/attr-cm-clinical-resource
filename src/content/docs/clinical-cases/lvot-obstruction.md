## Clinical question

Can cardiac amyloidosis present with clinically relevant left ventricular outflow tract obstruction and resemble hypertrophic obstructive cardiomyopathy?

## Published series

A 2025 JACC Case Reports series described **7 patients** with cardiac amyloidosis and concomitant LVOT obstruction who had initially been classified in an obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy pathway. The final amyloid diagnoses included **3 ATTR-CM and 4 AL cardiac amyloidosis** cases.

The reported delay between the initial HOCM classification and recognition of cardiac amyloidosis ranged from **0 to 34 months**.

## Why the series is useful

The series illustrates that LVOT obstruction does not by itself exclude cardiac amyloidosis. In older adults with an obstructive hypertrophic phenotype, the overall clinical context, extracardiac clues and disease trajectory may justify evaluation for amyloidosis.

## Important limitation

This is a small case series and should not be used to estimate prevalence. Its main value is **phenotype recognition and diagnostic awareness**, not incidence or treatment-effect estimation.
