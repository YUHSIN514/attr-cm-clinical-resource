# HCM × hereditary ATTR-CM coexistence

**2026｜*European Heart Journal - Case Reports***  
*Coexistence of hereditary transthyretin amyloid cardiomyopathy and sarcomeric hypertrophic cardiomyopathy: a multimodality imaging and genetic case report*
