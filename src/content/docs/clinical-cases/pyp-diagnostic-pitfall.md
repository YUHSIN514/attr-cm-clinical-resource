# Taiwan｜PYP diagnostic pitfall

**2023｜*European Heart Journal - Case Reports***  
*Pitfalls for the non-invasive diagnosis of wild-type transthyretin amyloid cardiomyopathy in a young adult: a case report*

30 歲男性具 LVH、relative apical sparing，且 **99mTc-PYP Grade 3**、無 monoclonal gammopathy、TTR genetic testing negative；因年齡與整體表現不典型進一步接受 CMR 與 endomyocardial biopsy，最終未證實 ATTR-CM。
